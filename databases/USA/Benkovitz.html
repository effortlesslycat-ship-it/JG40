Rabbi Icek Benkowitz of Chelsea, Massachusetts, Marriage Records: 1922-1954

Courtesy of the Rabbi's grandson, Leon Benkovitz. Transcriptions by Susan Posner and David Rosen of the Jewish Genealogy Society of Greater Boston.

This database contains 305 records from the Rabbi’s personal notebooks. Date and place of the marriage are given. Names, addresses, ages, occupations, color, places of birth, single/divorced/widowed, number of this marriage for brides and grooms are recorded. Parents names, and places of birth are recorded. There is a link to the image of the original record.    

 

Searching the Database

This database is searchable via the JewishGen Unified Search, and the JewishGen USA Collection.
