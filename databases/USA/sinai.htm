Sinai Congregation, Chicago, Marriages Database

Compiled by Fran Loeb Luebke

This is a database of information on 526 marriages, contained in two small books with handwritten notations attributed to Rabbi Bernhard Felsenthal.  Title: Marriage records, Sinai Congregation, Chicago, IL, records entered from 1861-1905.

The American Jewish Archives in Cincinnati, Ohio, holds the originals.  Database compiled from Manuscript SC #1858, Microfilm #3002, at AJA.  Permission to make data in these materials available on the internet was granted April 27, 1998.
