Colorado National Jewish Hospital Patient Applicants 1899-1914

Introduction by Ellen Shindelman Kowitt

Background

This ledger (1899-1914) is titled, "RECORD, ADMISSION & DISMISSION, NATIONAL JEWISH HOSPITAL FOR CONSUMPTIVES." Held at the Beck Archive at the University of Denver, the ledger was produced by W.H. Kistler, and is dated 1/21/1906 although records begin in 1899.

The data entry was completed by former DU employee Thyria Wilson and an unknown volunteer. Thanks to Thyria for notifying JGS Colorado about the register and to Beck Archivist David Fasman for sharing the spreadsheet.
 

Database

There are 3,976 entries in this collection, which include the following fields:

  • Surname
  • Given Name
  • Creed
  • Nativity
  • Date of Birth
  • Age at Hospital Registration
  • Residence City and State
  • Dates of Application, Admission, and Dismission
  • Report of Committee of Admission – whether admitted or rejected
  • Cause of dismission
  • Remarks by Committee
  • Who Recommended By
