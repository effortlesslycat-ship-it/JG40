Index of 1890 New York Immigrants from Austria, Poland, and Galicia

Compiled by Dr. Howard M. Relles

This is an index to all of the immigrants who arrived at the port of New York by ship in the year 1890, for whom the ship’s records indicated that they were citizens of Austria, Poland, or Galicia. There are a total of 44,052 records.
