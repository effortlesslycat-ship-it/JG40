El Paso, Texas Jewish Families
Introduction by Ellen Shindelman Kowitt

This names index, created by volunteer Sandy Aaronson, directs researchers to 645 individuals found in the narrative and family trees of the book, Insights and Hindsights of Some El Paso Jewish Families, which was published in 1983.

The index includes maiden names, birth and death dates and places, relationships to other individuals, occupations, Jewish institutional affiliations, and more.

It is always recommended to view the original material as indices do not contain all information. As there is no digitized version of the book available online at this time, it can be purchased or retrieved via. WorldCat through a local library or interlibrary loan. See Fierman, Floyd S. 1983. Insights and Hindsights of Some El Paso Jewish Families. El Paso Jewish Historical Society.
