Ships Arriving at the Port of New York, 1890-1891

In 1890 and 1891, there were a total of about 2,000 ship voyages per year which arrived at the port of New York, recorded in the National Archives microfilms.  Listed below are only the 1,375 voyages (671 in 1890, 704 in 1891) on which one or more citizens from Austria, Poland, or Galicia were on board.

The arrivals are listed below in alphabetical order by the name of the ship, and thereunder chronologically by date of arrival in New York.  Also listed in the table are the sequential ship number on the microfilm, the NARA microfilm reel number, and the number of Austrian/Polish/Galician passengers on board.
