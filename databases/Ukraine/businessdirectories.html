Ukraine Business Directories

Background

​The data come from a 1913 publication, Ves' Yugo-Zapadnyy Kray: spravochnaya i adresnaya kniga po Kievskoj, Podol’skoy i Voynskoy guberniyakh, a business directory of Kiev, Podolia, and Volyn provinces in the Russian Empire. 
This dataset contains more than 2,000 records culled from all the directory names that were clearly or possibly Jewish in these locations:
  1. Pogrebishche
  2. Zvenigorodka
  3. Zvenigorodka uezd (district)
  4. Tal’noye
  5. Mogilev-Podol’skiy.

The fields typically contain:

  • Family name and First Name(s)
  • Patronymic 
  • Business name
  • Street Address
  • Profession
  • City or Town Name
  • Other information (which includes comments)
The directory contains descriptions of the relevant cities and towns, and also a link to the directory file, which is at the National Library of Ukraine in Kiev.
